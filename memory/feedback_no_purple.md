Russell stated flatly "I hate purple" while reviewing the makobytes.com / PromptPixel hero (2026-04-09). The design I shipped leaned heavily on violet (`#8b5cf6`) as the primary accent alongside cyan and magenta.

**Why:** Personal color preference. Strong enough reaction ("hate") that it should be treated as a hard rule for this project, not a soft nudge.

**How to apply:**
- Default PromptPixel / makobytes palette away from violet. Replace with blue (cyan/electric blue) + magenta accents, or pure monochrome + cyan.
- Any future "dark cinematic" design I pitch for Russell should NOT default to violet unless he explicitly asks.
- Applies site-wide, not just to Whobee. Check: btn-glow, text-gradient-violet, glow-violet ring colors, step icon, FAQ plus icon, grid overlay tint, testimonial avatar gradients, footer mark, pricing card glow ring, etc.
- Worth checking if this applies to other projects too — likely yes, but confirm when a new project comes up.
